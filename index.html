About STELLUNEX & References

🎯 Project Purpose

STELLUNEX is built as an interactive 3D Digital Astronomy Museum to provide accurate celestial coordinates, stellar structures, and deep-sky astronomical data based on scientific standards.

📚 Scientific Data Sources

  • IAU (International Astronomical Union): Standard 88 constellation boundaries & coordinates
  • SIMBAD Astronomical Database (CDS, Strasbourg): Spectral types, apparent magnitudes, and light year distances
  • NASA / IPAC Extragalactic Database (NED): Deep-Sky Messier and NGC objects
  • ESA Gaia Mission: Precise star coordinates on the celestial sphere
